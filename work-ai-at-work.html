The problem

Everyone has AI now. Almost no one was trained on it.

Companies are rolling out tools like ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude, and employees are learning by trial and error. The result: vague prompts and weak answers, sensitive data pasted into public tools, and AI mistakes passed along unchecked. These employees don't need to know how AI works. They need a few habits they can use the same day.

Design decisions

What makes it work

Built for busy, skeptical learners

  • One memorable framework: Goal, Context, Format, Check. It fits on a sticky note.
  • A story, not slides: learners follow Maya through a real workday.
  • Practice on almost every screen: sorting, building a prompt, share-or-don't decisions, and finding errors in an AI-written email.
  • Feedback that explains why, not just right or wrong.

Built to be used, and measured

  • Accessible: buttons instead of drag-and-drop, keyboard navigation, screen-reader announcements, readable contrast.
  • Works on any device, and remembers where learners left off.
  • Performance support: a printable one-page job aid.
  • Evaluation plan across Kirkpatrick Levels 1–4.
