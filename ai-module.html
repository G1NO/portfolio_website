Using AI at Work: Quick Guide

Keep this near your desk.
GGoal
Say exactly what you want.
CContext
Give it what it needs to know.
FFormat
Length, tone, structure.
CCheck
Verify before you use it.

Good fit

  • Drafting emails and documents
  • Summarizing your own notes or long text
  • Changing tone, brainstorming, simplifying

Be careful

  • Exact facts, figures, prices and recent events
  • Math (use a spreadsheet)
  • Decisions about people

Keep out of AI tools

  • Customer personal info, passwords, confidential financials, unreleased plans, HR or medical info
  • Test: would I be OK if this showed up outside the company?
  • Use placeholders like [Customer] and [Account #], and follow your company's AI policy

The 3-step check

  • Facts: verify names, numbers, dates and policies against a real source
  • Fit: does it say what you mean, in the right tone?
  • Final read: read it as the person receiving it
